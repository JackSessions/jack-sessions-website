Presented at the **UN World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) Forum 2025** in Geneva, Switzerland.

The talk addressed AI safety from a practitioner security perspective: the gap between academic AI safety discourse and the realities of deploying AI systems in adversarial environments, responsible publication norms for offensive AI capabilities, and what dual-use research means when the researcher is 22 years old and the audience is international policymakers.

Key themes covered:

- The dual-use research problem applied to AI: when publishing attack capabilities creates risk vs. when withholding them does
- LLM manipulation, prompt injection, and quantisation attacks as categories of practitioner concern that academic AI safety frameworks often miss
- What responsible disclosure looks like for AI vulnerabilities given the absence of coordinated disclosure norms equivalent to those in traditional security research
- The policy gap: why the people writing AI safety governance are often not the people actually breaking AI systems, and what that means for the resulting frameworks
